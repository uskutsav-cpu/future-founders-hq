FUTURE FOUNDERS — YOUR FIRST 60 MINUTES
Draft chapter resource

BEFORE THE MEETING
Book an approved space. Arrange seats so people can talk. Bring paper and markers. Choose a way to share the next meeting details that follows school policies.

00–10: Introductions
Name, one thing you are curious about, and one everyday problem you noticed this week.

10–20: Problem swap
In pairs, choose one problem. Who experiences it? What do they do today? Avoid jumping straight to a solution.

20–40: Make something small
Sketch a first solution. Create a paper prototype, an interview script, or a simple landing page concept.

40–50: Show and ask
Give each team two minutes to share. Ask: what assumption would you test first?

50–60: One next step
Each person chooses a small action before the next meeting. Share the next date and invite volunteers to help organize it.

AFTERWARD
Write down what worked, what felt slow, and what to change. Invite feedback from someone who spoke less.
