FUTURE FOUNDERS — CHAPTER NAMING
Draft brand guidance for review before launch.

Preferred name: Future Founders at [Verified School Name]
Short display: [Verified School Name] Chapter

Use a school's name only after authorization and chapter approval are confirmed. Follow the school's branding and organization policies. Do not use a university or school logo without permission.

Keep Future Founders as two words. Use the shared FF mark as supplied and maintain clear space around it. Keep text readable; avoid adding effects or stretching the logo.

Brand palette:
Paper #F7F6F0
Ink #20231F
Vermilion #DA3929
Light sage #E7EBDF
Citrus #E5F19A

Use ink for body text and vermilion for important actions. Verify contrast when placing text on a colored surface.
