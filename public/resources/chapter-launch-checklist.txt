FUTURE FOUNDERS — CHAPTER LAUNCH CHECKLIST
Draft planning resource • September 2026

1. Write down the problem your chapter will solve for students at your school.
2. Identify a small founding team. Assign responsibility for programs, community, and operations.
3. Review your school's student organization requirements. Find an advisor if required.
4. Complete the Future Founders application and confirm affiliation before using the chapter name publicly.
5. Complete school approval. Confirm room booking, event rules, and any safeguarding requirements with your school.
6. Talk to ten potential members. Ask what they want to build or learn.
7. Plan a first meeting with one useful activity and one clear next step.
8. Choose a regular meeting time and a school-approved communication channel.
9. Publish a simple four-week calendar and invite your first members personally.
10. After your first month, ask what worked. Document the plan and prepare the next student leaders.

School rules and network requirements must be confirmed directly. This checklist does not grant chapter approval.
